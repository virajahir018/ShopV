# ShopV - User Guide

## 🎯 Getting Started

Welcome to **ShopV**, a fully functional e-commerce website built with Next.js. This guide will help you navigate and use all the features.

---

## 📱 Main Features

### 1. **Home Page**
- **Hero Slider**: Browse featured banners with auto-rotation
- **Shop by Category**: Explore 8 different product categories
- **Top Brands**: View popular brands
- **Deals of the Day**: Check out special offers
- **Featured Products**: Browse handpicked products

### 2. **Product Browsing**
- **View All Products**: Click "View All" to see complete product listing
- **Product Details**: Click any product to view full details
- **Product Information**: 
  - Title and Brand
  - Price, Original Price, and Discount
  - Rating and Reviews (Stars)
  - Delivery Information

### 3. **Shopping Cart**
- **Add to Cart**: Select quantity (1-10) and add product
- **View Cart**: Click "Bag" in navbar to view cart
- **Manage Cart**:
  - Increase/decrease quantity using +/- buttons
  - Remove individual items
  - View grand total
  - Proceed to checkout

### 4. **Wishlist**
- **Add to Wishlist**: Click heart icon on any product
- **View Wishlist**: Click "Wishlist" in navbar
- **Remove from Wishlist**: Click heart icon again to remove
- **Empty Wishlist Message**: Message when no items are saved

### 5. **Search**
- **Search Products**: Use search bar in navbar
- **Search by**: Product title or brand name
- **Real-time**: Results update as you type

### 6. **Filtering & Sorting**
- **Filter by Category**: Men, Women, Kids, Footwear, Beauty, Watches, Bags, Sports
- **Filter by Brand**: Multiple brand selection available
- **Filter by Price**: Adjust price range slider (₹500 - ₹10,000)
- **Filter by Rating**: Select minimum rating (1★, 2★, 3★, 4★)
- **Sort Options**:
  - Default
  - Price: Low to High
  - Price: High to Low
  - Highest Rated
- **Clear Filters**: Reset all filters with one click

### 7. **Checkout**
- **Enter Details**:
  - Full Name
  - Phone Number
  - Delivery Address
  - City and Pincode
- **Select Payment Method**:
  - Cash on Delivery (COD)
  - UPI
  - Credit/Debit Card
- **Order Summary**: Review items and total
- **Place Order**: Confirm and place order

### 8. **Order Confirmation**
- **Order Success Page**: Confirmation with Order ID
- **Continue Shopping**: Return to home page

### 9. **Product Management** (For Sellers)
- **Add Product**:
  - Fill product details
  - Enter price, discount, rating
  - Provide product image URL
  - Submit to add to store
- **Edit Product**:
  - Click "Edit" on your product
  - Modify details
  - Click "Update Product"
- **Delete Product**:
  - Click "Delete" on your product
  - Confirm deletion
  - Product removed from store
- **View Your Products**: See all products you've added at bottom of page

## 🔍 Detailed Usage Instructions

### How to Search for a Product

1. Go to the homepage
2. Click on the search bar at the top
3. Type product name, brand, or category
4. Results appear automatically
5. Click on a product to view details

### How to Filter Products

1. Navigate to "All Products" page
2. On the left sidebar, you'll see filter options:
   - **Category**: Check desired categories
   - **Brand**: Select preferred brands
   - **Max Price**: Drag slider to set budget
   - **Rating**: Select minimum rating
3. Products update automatically
4. Click "Clear" to reset all filters

### How to Add Product to Cart

1. Find the product you want
2. Click on the product card
3. On the product details page:
   - Select quantity using dropdown (1-10)
   - Click "Add To Cart"
   - Confirmation message appears
4. Product added with selected quantity

### How to Checkout

1. Go to Shopping Cart (click "Bag" in navbar)
2. Review your items
3. Click "Proceed to Checkout"
4. Fill in your details:
   - Name, Phone, Address
   - City and Pincode
5. Select payment method
6. Review order summary
7. Click "Place Order"
8. Order confirmation page appears

### How to Manage Wishlist

1. **Add to Wishlist**: Click heart icon on any product
2. **View Wishlist**: Click "Wishlist" in navbar
3. **Remove**: Click heart icon again
4. **View Product**: Click product card to view details
5. **Add from Wishlist**: Click "Add to Cart" on product page

### How to Add/Edit/Delete Products

1. Click "Add Product" in navbar
2. **To Add**:
   - Fill all form fields
   - Click "Add Product" button
   - Product appears in your list
3. **To Edit**:
   - Scroll to "Your Added Products"
   - Click "Edit" on desired product
   - Form populates with product details
   - Modify and click "Update Product"
4. **To Delete**:
   - Click "Delete" on product
   - Confirm deletion
   - Product removed from store

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Will my cart be saved if I close the browser?**
A: Yes! Your cart and wishlist are saved automatically using local storage.

**Q: Can I edit products after adding?**
A: Yes! Go to "Add Product" page, scroll to your products, and click "Edit".

**Q: What happens if I forget to fill a form field?**
A: You'll see an alert reminding you to fill all required fields.

**Q: Can I change payment method after checkout?**
A: You can select payment method during checkout. Once order is placed, contact support.

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Currently, this is a demo application. In production, proper security measures will be implemented.

**Q: How do I filter by multiple categories?**
A: Check multiple category checkboxes - products will filter to show items from all selected categories.

**Q: Can I sort filtered results?**
A: Yes! Sorting works with any filter combination.

**Q: How long does delivery take?**
A: This is a demo application. In production, delivery timeline will be displayed.
